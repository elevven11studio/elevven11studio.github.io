How to Get More Customers on WhatsApp Business

23 Nov 2026 · 4 min read

For many small businesses in Nigeria, WhatsApp is where most sales happen. Customers already have it, they trust it and they reply fast. But a lot of businesses use it the same way they chat with friends, and that leaves sales on the table.

These are the small changes that make your WhatsApp work more like a shop counter.

Use the Business app, not your personal one

WhatsApp Business is a separate free app made for businesses. It gives you tools the normal app does not, like a business profile, a catalogue and automatic messages.

If you can, put it on a separate number from your personal line. Customers then reach the business, not you at 11pm. Keep the number the same for a long time. Every time it changes, you lose people who saved the old one.

Fill in your business profile

The profile is the first thing a curious customer checks. Fill in every field the app offers:

  • Business name exactly as it appears on your signboard.
  • Category, so people know what you do at a glance.
  • Description in one or two plain sentences. "We sell and repair phones in Wuse 2" beats a list of slogans.
  • Address, if customers can visit.
  • Opening hours.
  • Email and website, if you have them.

Use a clear profile photo, such as your logo or shopfront. A blurry picture makes a business look temporary.

Build a catalogue

The catalogue lets customers browse your products inside WhatsApp, with photos, names, prices and short descriptions. It saves you from sending the same pictures again and again.

Tips that help:

  • Show the price. "DM for price" makes many people leave without asking.
  • Use good photos. See how to take better product photos with your phone.
  • Remove sold-out items, or mark them clearly. Nothing annoys a customer more than choosing something that is gone.
  • Answer the usual questions in the description, like size, colour or what is included.

Set up quick replies and automatic messages

You answer the same questions every day: price, location, delivery, account number. Quick replies let you save those answers and send them with a short keyword instead of typing them each time.

Good quick replies to save:

  1. Your location and directions, or a map link.
  2. Delivery areas and fees.
  3. How to pay and how to confirm payment.
  4. Opening hours.

The app also lets you set a greeting message for new chats and an away message for when you are closed. An away message that says when you will reply is far better than silence.

Post on status with a purpose

Your status is seen by people who have saved your number. It is free advertising to people who already know you, so use it.

What works well:

  • New stock or restocks, with prices.
  • A short video of the product in use.
  • A happy customer's message, with their permission and their details hidden.
  • A clear reason to reply today, like a limited quantity.

Post regularly, but do not flood it. Twenty posts in a row makes people tap through without looking.

Broadcast without being a nuisance

Broadcast lists send one message to many contacts at once, and each person gets it as a private chat. A contact only receives your broadcast if they have saved your number, so ask customers to save it.

People who feel spammed will block you, so follow a few rules:

  • Only add people who have bought from you or asked to hear from you.
  • Send useful messages, not daily ones. New stock or an offer is fine. "Good morning" from a business is not.
  • Keep it short and put the important part first.
  • Make it easy to opt out. "Reply STOP and we will remove you" is enough, and respect it.

Link your WhatsApp everywhere

Make it easy to start a chat. WhatsApp supports click to chat links in the form https://wa.me/234XXXXXXXXXX, using your full number with the country code and without the plus sign or the first zero.

Put that link:

  • In your Instagram and Facebook bios.
  • On your Google Business Profile.
  • On flyers and your shop counter as a QR code. Our free QR code generator makes one in your browser.
  • On your website, as a button that is easy to tap on a phone.

Why a website helps WhatsApp sales

WhatsApp is great for chatting, but customers who find you on Google need somewhere to land first. A website shows your products, prices and location, then sends people to WhatsApp ready to buy.

Every Elevven11 website package includes a WhatsApp button, and the Store package lets customers fill a cart and send the whole order to your WhatsApp with the total and delivery fee. See the pricing page, or read how to sell on WhatsApp with your own online store.

Reply fast and follow up once

All of this brings people in. What turns them into customers is speed. Answer within minutes during your hours. If someone asks about an item and goes quiet, one polite follow-up the next day is fair. More than that starts to feel like pressure.

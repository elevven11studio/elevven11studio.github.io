# How to Earn Commission Referring Website Clients

How a website referral programme works, who makes a good referral, what you can earn, and the rules that keep commissions fair.

You probably know several people who need a website. The tailor who only has an Instagram page. The caterer who sends price lists as screenshots. The tutor whose parents keep asking for details. Referral programmes let you earn from those connections without building anything yourself. This guide explains how they work, who to approach, and what to watch for.

## How website referral programmes work

The idea is simple. A web studio gives you a way to send customers to it, usually a code or a link. When someone you sent becomes a paying customer, you get a commission.

You do not need to design, code or explain technical details. Your job is the introduction. The studio handles the quote, the build, the communication and the support.

## Who makes a good referral

The best referrals are people who already sell something and are losing time or customers without a website. Look for:

- Small shops that sell through WhatsApp or Instagram DMs
- Barbers, stylists and beauty businesses
- Caterers, bakers and small restaurants
- Photographers and event planners
- Tutors, consultants and coaches
- Freelancers who need a portfolio
- Local service providers like plumbers, cleaners and AC technicians

A good sign is someone who keeps sending the same information to every new customer. A website answers those questions once.

## How to start the conversation

You do not need a sales pitch. Try something closer to this:

1. Notice the problem. "You answer the price question all day, don't you?"
2. Offer a simple fix. "A small site with your prices and a WhatsApp button would save you that."
3. Show, don't tell. Send them a demo that matches their business from the [examples page](https://elevven11studio.github.io/examples/).
4. Hand over. Give them your referral link and let the studio take it from there.

Keep it honest. Do not promise features, prices or timelines the studio has not confirmed.

## How tracking works

Each agent gets a unique referral code. During this early phase, tracking is manual: you message us to get your code, then share a link like this with customers:

`https://elevven11studio.github.io/get-started/?ref=YOUR-CODE`

When a customer opens your link and submits the Get Started form, your code is attached to their message automatically, so the referral is credited to you.

Always send people your own link rather than the plain website address. A referral that cannot be traced to you cannot be paid.

## The rules

Every fair programme has rules. Ours are short:

- Each agent receives a unique referral code.
- The referral must be attributable to the agent.
- Commission is paid after customer payment is confirmed.
- Agents should not promise features or prices outside the official packages.
- Elevven11 Studio handles final pricing and technical decisions.
- Fraudulent or duplicate referrals do not qualify.
- Commission rates may be updated for future referrals, with notice.

Agents are independent referrers, not employees.

## Questions to ask any referral programme

If you are comparing programmes, check these:

1. When exactly is commission paid: on signup, on payment, or on completion?
2. How is the referral tracked, and what if the customer forgets to use my link?
3. Can rates change, and do changes affect referrals already made?
4. Who handles the customer after the introduction?

A programme with clear answers is one you can recommend without worrying about your reputation.

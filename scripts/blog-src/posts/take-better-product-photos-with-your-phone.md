Online, the photo is the product. A customer cannot pick it up, feel it or try it on. If the picture is dark, blurry or cluttered, they assume the item is too, and they scroll past.

You do not need a camera or a studio. A recent phone, a window and a little care will give you photos that sell. Here is what makes the biggest difference, in order.

## 1. Get the light right

Light matters more than your phone. A cheap phone in good light beats an expensive one in a dark room.

- **Use daylight from a window.** Place the product near a window, with the light coming from the side. Mid-morning and afternoon light is softer than midday sun.
- **Avoid direct sun.** Harsh sun makes hard shadows and bright patches. If the sun is shining straight in, hang a thin white curtain or bedsheet over the window to soften it.
- **Turn off the room lights.** Mixing yellow bulbs with daylight gives strange colours.
- **Do not use the flash.** It flattens the product and creates shine on glass, plastic and packaging.

If one side of the product is too dark, hold a sheet of white paper or cardboard on that side. It bounces light back and fills the shadow.

## 2. Keep the background plain

A busy background pulls attention away from what you are selling. Bedsheets with patterns, a messy table or people walking past all make the photo look rushed.

Cheap options that work:

- A large sheet of white cardboard, curved from the wall down onto the table so there is no visible corner.
- A plain wall and a plain table.
- A clean wooden surface or fabric in one colour, for food, crafts or fashion.

White and light grey suit most products. Pick one background and stick with it.

## 3. Hold the phone steady

Blur usually comes from movement, not a bad camera.

- Wipe the lens. Phones live in pockets, and a greasy lens makes everything look hazy.
- Hold the phone with both hands, or rest it on a stack of books.
- Tap the screen on the product to focus before you take the shot.
- Take several photos and keep the sharpest.

## 4. Shoot more than one angle

One photo rarely answers every question. Show what a customer would check in person:

1. **The front**, straight on, filling most of the frame.
2. **A side or back view.**
3. **A close-up** of the detail that matters, such as stitching, a label, a texture or ports on a gadget.
4. **The item in use or next to something familiar**, so people understand the size. A bag on a shoulder or a jar next to a hand works better than a description.

Shoot at the product's level, not from far above. For flat items like fabric, books or plates, a top-down shot works well.

## 5. Stay consistent

When every product photo uses the same background, light and angle, your page or catalogue looks like a real shop. Mixed styles look like a pile of forwarded pictures.

Make a simple routine and repeat it:

- Same spot by the same window, at about the same time of day.
- Same background.
- Same distance and angle for the main photo.
- Same shape. Square photos are a safe choice for catalogues, social media and most online stores.

## 6. Edit lightly

Your phone's built-in editor is enough. Good edits are small:

- **Crop and straighten** so the product sits in the middle and edges are level.
- **Brighten a little** if the photo looks dull.
- **Adjust warmth** if white looks yellow or blue.

Avoid heavy filters and do not change the product's colour. If a customer orders a red dress and receives a maroon one, you have a return and an unhappy customer. The photo should match what arrives.

## 7. Take the photos people forget

Some pictures build trust without showing the product itself:

- The packaging, so customers know how it arrives.
- Your shopfront or workspace, for your Google listing and website.
- Labels and sizes, so you answer questions before they are asked.

## Quick checklist before you post

| Check | Why |
|---|---|
| Lit by daylight, no flash | True colour and soft shadows |
| Plain background | Product stands out |
| Sharp when zoomed in | Customers zoom in |
| Two or more angles | Fewer questions |
| Same style as your other photos | Looks like a real shop |
| Colour matches the real item | Fewer returns |

Agent Rules

Keep it simple

  • Each agent receives a unique referral code.
  • The referral must be attributable to the agent.
  • Commission is paid after customer payment is confirmed.
  • Agents should not promise features or prices outside the official packages.
  • Elevven11 Studio handles final pricing and technical decisions.
  • Fraudulent or duplicate referrals do not qualify for commission.
  • Commission rates may be updated for future referrals, with notice.

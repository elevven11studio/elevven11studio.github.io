Why

What this is, and what it isn't

Elevven11 Studio is self-funded. The template demos, the pricing breakdown and the FAQ are all free to read, and there is no account to make and nothing behind a paywall. Keeping that online costs a little every month, and this page is where anyone who wants to help with that can.

It is not a payment for work. If you are hiring the studio to build you a website, that runs through Get Started at the prices on the pricing page and has nothing to do with this page. Chipping in here does not move you up a queue, buy a discount, or come with anything attached.

Donations are not tax-deductible and are not refundable. Every payment is processed by Paystack, which handles the card details and issues the receipt.

Free Alternatives

Other ways to help

Money is the least interesting way to support a small studio, and these cost nothing:

  • Send the site to someone who needs a website. If you want a commission for it, the referral programme pays one.
  • Follow or share a post on Instagram, Facebook or LinkedIn.
  • Tell us what is missing or wrong on the site. Corrections are genuinely useful, and the contact form reaches us quickly.

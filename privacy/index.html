Privacy

Plain language, no legal jargon: here's what happens to your information.

What we collect

When you fill in the Get Started form or the Contact form, we collect what you type: your name, phone/WhatsApp number, email, business details, and anything else you choose to share (like a referral code from an agent).

This site also uses Google Analytics to see basic, aggregated traffic information — pages visited, rough location (country/city level), and device type. It doesn't identify you personally.

Where it goes

This is a static site with no database. Submitting the Get Started or Contact forms opens WhatsApp with your details pre-filled — the message only reaches us when you press send inside WhatsApp. We don't receive or store anything until that message arrives in our WhatsApp chat.

If you arrived via a referral link (a URL ending in ?ref=CODE), that code is held briefly in your browser (not sent to any server) so it can be included in your WhatsApp message and credited to the right referral agent.

Who sees it

Only Elevven11 Studio sees the information you send via WhatsApp or email. We don't sell or share your details with third parties. Google Analytics processes anonymized traffic data under its own privacy policy.

Questions

If you want any information you've sent us deleted, or have a question about this, message us on WhatsApp or email elevven11studio@gmail.com.

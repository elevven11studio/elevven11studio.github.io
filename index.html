Common questions

Do I really pay no hosting fee?

Eligible static websites are hosted on GitHub Pages, which lets us provide hosting without charging a monthly hosting fee. Sites needing a database, logins, or server-side processing may need a different hosting setup, quoted separately.

What counts as a free update?

Text changes, image swaps, and updating your phone number, WhatsApp, or social links. New pages, redesigns, and new functionality are quoted separately.

How long does delivery take?

Most Starter and Plus websites are built quickly once we have your content, thanks to our reusable template system.

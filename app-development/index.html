Pricing

Why there is no price on this page

Our website packages have fixed prices because the work is genuinely similar every time. Apps are not like that. An app that shows your menu and opening hours is a different job from one where customers make accounts, pay inside the app and get notifications, and putting a single number on both would mean overcharging one of them.

So we scope first and price second. Tell us what the app needs to do, we come back with a fixed price and a timeline in writing, and that price does not move once you have agreed to it. If the answer is that an app is the wrong tool and a website would serve you better, we will say so.

What moves the number

Pushes it up

  • User accounts and logins
  • Payments taken inside the app
  • A server or database behind it
  • Push notifications
  • Working offline and syncing later
  • Maps, chat or live tracking
  • An admin panel for you to manage content

Keeps it down

  • A small, clear set of screens
  • Content you can already supply
  • Existing branding we can work from
  • An existing website or API to connect to
  • Launching on one store first
  • Knowing what the app is for before we start

Quoted and invoiced in Naira, the same as our website work.

The Fine Print

What's in, and what's billed separately

Included in the quote

  • Screen design and the app icon
  • The Flutter build for Android and iOS
  • Test builds you can install before launch
  • Store listing text and screenshots
  • Submission to Google Play and the App Store
  • 1 month of free fixes after launch

Billed separately, at cost

  • Google Play developer registration (one-off)
  • Apple Developer Program (yearly)
  • Server, database or push notification services
  • Payment provider fees
  • New features after launch
  • Paid fonts, stock photos or icons you choose

A month of free fixes covers things that are broken, not things that are new. Features nobody scoped are quoted on their own, the same way our website update policy works.

Questions

Before you ask

Why is there no price on this page?

Because scope is the cost. A five-screen app that shows information is a different amount of work from one with accounts, payments and a server behind it, and putting one number on both would mean overcharging somebody. We scope your app first, then give you a fixed price in writing before anything starts.

Do I need two separate apps for Android and iPhone?

No. Flutter builds both from one codebase, so you pay for one build rather than two, and a change you ask for later lands on both platforms at once.

Is there a monthly fee for an app?

Not to us for the app itself. But an app is not a static website, so the free GitHub Pages hosting that covers our website packages does not apply here. If your app needs a server, a database or push notifications, those run on paid services and we will tell you what they cost before you commit. Google Play charges a one-off developer registration fee and Apple charges a yearly one.

How long does an app take?

Longer than a website. We give you a timeline with the quote, once we know what the app has to do. Store review adds time at the end that is outside our control, particularly on Apple.

Can you publish it under my own store account?

Yes, and we recommend it. The account should be in your business name so the app stays yours. We will walk you through creating it and handle the submission itself.

Can you build an app for a website you already made me?

Yes. If we built your site we already have your branding and content, which takes some of the design work off the quote.

General FAQ

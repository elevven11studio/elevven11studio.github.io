What It Does

Built for paper-heavy offices

Documents

Folders, categories, tags and full version history. A new upload never replaces the old one. Search reads titles, notes, file names and the text on scans.

Paper archive

Rooms, cabinets, shelves and boxes. Check a file out to a person with a return date, and see what is overdue or missing. Print QR labels and scan them back.

Scanning

Photograph pages or pick images, reorder them and save one PDF. Text on the pages is read on the device, offline, and added to search.

Tasks and approvals

Assign work with due dates and priority. Approvals go to a named approver, and nobody approves their own work.

Letters and contacts

A numbered register of letters in and out, replies linked to what they answer, and alerts when a reply is late.

Forms, notices and meetings

Build forms and review answers. Post notices with read confirmations. Keep agendas, minutes and action items, all on one calendar.

Modules

Switch on only what you use

Every module turns on and off per workspace. Turning one off hides it and keeps its records.

People and HR

Employee records, leave, attendance, equipment and certificates with expiry dates. Hiring from applicant to employee, with joining and leaving checklists.

Payroll

PAYE on the Nigeria Tax Act 2025 bands, pension, NHF and payslips. One person prepares, another approves. Bank schedules and remittance files export.

Invoices and books

Numbered invoices and receipts, VAT, expenses and fixed assets, all posted to double entry books. Import a bank statement and match it.

Clinic

Patients, visits, prescriptions, lab tests, appointments, wards, drug rounds, stock and HMO claims. It records what clinicians decide and suggests nothing.

Property and rent

Properties, units, landlords and tenancies. Rent is invoiced period by period, with repairs tracked from reported to done.

Shared logins

Bank portals, tax and email logins, encrypted for each person they are shared with. A copied password clears after 30 seconds.

Office Network

One office, many devices

One computer hosts

The main computer shares the workspace over an encrypted connection on the office network. No cloud service sits in between.

Others pair and sync

Other PCs pair with a QR code or a one-time code. They keep working offline and catch up when they reconnect.

Security and Backup

Your records stay yours

There is no WorkNest server. Elevven11 holds no copy of your records or keys.

Encrypted on the device

The database is encrypted with SQLCipher (AES-256), with its key in the operating system's secure storage.

PINs never stored

Only a salted Argon2id hash is kept. Five wrong tries lock the person out, and the app locks itself after 15 minutes.

Verified backups

A .wnbak file holds records and files together, sealed with the recovery phrase or a password. Back up on a schedule, with a copy to USB.

Tamper-evident log

Every action is chained, so a changed or missing entry is detected and reported. Archive movements can never be edited.

Restricted records

Restricted folders, HR, payroll and patient records are invisible to anyone without the permission, including in search and exports.

Files checked on read

Each file is stored once by its SHA-256 inside the encrypted database and checked every time it is opened.

FAQ

Common questions

Do I need internet or an account?

No. WorkNest runs fully offline on your own computers, and devices share over your office network.

Can Elevven11 see our records?

No. Nothing is sent to us. There are no ads, analytics or tracking in the app.

Which devices does it run on?

Windows first. Android phones and tablets, iPhone and iPad follow.

Are the payroll figures tax advice?

No. WorkNest works out pay from the rates you set. Have an accountant check them before you rely on them.

What if a computer is stolen?

Install WorkNest on a new computer and restore your latest backup with the recovery phrase or backup password.

How much does it cost?

Pricing is per organization and number of devices. Message us and we will send it.

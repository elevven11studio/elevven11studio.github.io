What It Does

Everything a shop counts

Selling

Tap, search or scan. Split payments, held baskets, discounts, voids and refunds. Receipts print on 58 mm or 80 mm paper, or share as an image, PDF or link.

Products and stock

Variants, barcodes, units by weight or volume and low stock warnings. Stock only moves through recorded entries, and profit uses your average cost.

Customers and credit

Credit limits, balances, payments and store credit. See at a glance who owes you and who you owe.

Suppliers and orders

Raise purchase orders, receive goods at their real cost and track what you owe each supplier.

Shifts and the drawer

Open with a float, close with a count. Shortages and overages are recorded, not hidden.

Reports

Revenue, gross and net profit, sales by hour, stock value and rankings for products, staff and payment methods.

Digital receipts

Every receipt carries a QR code. A customer scans it and sees a copy on their phone, with no app and no customer or staff name on it. Nothing is stored online. Also included: expenses, quotes and invoices, import from CSV or Excel, export to Excel, CSV or JSON, ready-made products for nine kinds of shop, and light, dark and custom colours.

Activity Log

Built to catch mistakes and theft

A watch list

Voids, refunds, large discounts, price changes, stock losses, no-sale drawer opens, repeated wrong PINs and receipt reprints.

Tamper detection

Entries are chained together, so changing or deleting an old one is detected and reported. Entries cannot be edited in the app.

More Than One Device

One shop, several tills

Web till

Turn on sharing and any phone, tablet or computer on the shop Wi-Fi can sell from a browser. Each person signs in with their own PIN.

Join a business

Install Counterbook on a second device and pair it with a code. Devices catch up whenever they reconnect, and offline changes are merged.

Syncing works on one network. There is no cloud service in between.

Security and Backup

Your data stays yours

Counterbook keeps everything on your device. Elevven11 holds no copy of your data or keys.

Encrypted on the device

The business database is encrypted with SQLCipher (AES-256). The key sits in Windows Credential Manager or the Android Keystore.

PINs never stored

Only a salted Argon2id hash is kept. Repeated wrong PINs lock the person out for longer each time.

Encrypted backups

A .cbbak file holds everything, including staff logins. Back up on a schedule, to a USB drive or your own Google Drive.

Permissions enforced

Every action is checked in the data layer, so a role without a permission is refused even if a screen were reached.

Safe exports

Cells that start with =, +, - or @ are neutralised, so a spreadsheet cannot run them as formulas.

No internet of its own

The app makes no connections except to Google Drive, and only if you connect it.

No reset link

If the device and the recovery phrase are both lost and there is no password-protected backup, the data cannot be recovered by anyone. Keep the phrase on paper, away from the device, and test a restore once a month.

FAQ

Common questions

Do I need an account or internet?

No. Counterbook works fully offline. The internet is only used if you connect Google Drive for backups.

Is it really free?

Yes. It shows one small, labelled promotion at the foot of the More screen, never on the till, and a setting turns it off.

Which devices does it run on?

Windows and Android as apps, and any phone, tablet or computer on your shop Wi-Fi through the web till.

Why does the profit look too high?

Cost price is probably zero. Starter products start at zero cost, so enter the cost when you receive stock.

What if my laptop is stolen?

Install Counterbook on a new device and restore your latest backup with the recovery phrase or backup password. Staff logins come back with it.

Is there cloud sync over the internet?

Not yet. For different places, move data with a backup file or Google Drive.
